Good decisions start with clear information.

This educational dashboard uses Yahoo Finance's GC=F gold futures feed. Futures prices differ from XAU/USD spot prices and jewellery shop prices. Quotes may be delayed; an older timestamp can also mean the market is closed. No synthetic prices are displayed.

How fresh is the data?

Quotes are checked every 60 seconds and history every hour. The provider supplies 5-minute bars. Quote time is the bar's timestamp, not a guaranteed real-time trade. Failed refreshes retain the last successful quote and display a warning.

What happened to the machine-learning forecast?

The original XGBoost model had a high holdout error, so it is not promoted as a reliable public forecast. The outlook here is a transparent historical-volatility scenario, not that model. The experimental model remains available through the API.
